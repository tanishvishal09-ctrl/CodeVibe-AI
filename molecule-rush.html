🎓 How to Play Molecule Rush 2.0

🎯 Objective

Match molecules with their correct properties to score points and advance through levels!

🎮 Gameplay

• A molecule and its formula will be displayed
• Choose the correct answer from the options
• Earn points for correct answers
• Lose a life for wrong answers

⚡ Difficulty

• Easy (Levels 1-3): Basic molecules
• Medium (Levels 4-7): Intermediate challenges
• Hard (Levels 8+): Expert level molecules
• Difficulty increases smoothly as you progress

💡 Tips

• You start with 3 lives (❤️)
• Correct answers: +10 points
• Use the pause button anytime
• Adjust settings for better experience

♿ Accessibility

• Colorblind mode available in settings
• Sound can be adjusted or muted
• Works on both desktop and mobile
